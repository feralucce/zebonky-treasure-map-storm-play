Test play

Click the game, then: arrows run, Space or Z jumps, touch food and paws to take them, Down opens crates, Up goes through doors, X or F tosses food, R restarts.

    Guides for the artist

    Full-size sheets made from this level, at 2x game size. Put one on its own layer in your paint app, paint on a layer under it, and hide the sheet before saving the art. PNG is see-through where nothing is marked; JPG is on white.

    Artist's files

    Drop what the artist sends back, as it is: a background, and dressing painted on the whole-level guide. The guide is taken out of the dressing, a repeating background is cut to one copy, and test play shows them with the pixel ground.
